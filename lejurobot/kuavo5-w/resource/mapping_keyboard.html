Mapping keyboard control

Limits: 0.15 m/s forward/reverse · 0.3 rad/s turning · 300 ms heartbeat timeout.

Start mapping first. This panel uses the factory MANUAL RobotMotion route. Other base controllers remain paused or blocked. Opening this panel does not enable motion.

Keep this panel focused. Hold Alt and a direction, or hold a direction button. Release the key/button to stop. Switching tabs or leaving this panel releases control. After preparation completes, release all keys and press again.
